Privacy

Your voice never leaves your phone.

Not a policy promise bolted on afterwards — it is how the app is built. Recognition happens on the device, so there is no upload to opt out of.

  • Audio stays on-device Speech recognition runs locally. Recordings are never uploaded or stored off your phone.
  • No account, no server There is nothing to sign up for. Your notes and photos live in your phone's own storage.
  • Cloud organizing is optional When on, only transcribed text is sent — never audio or photos. Turn it off and an on-device rule engine takes over.
  • Works with no signal Capture, transcribe and search all work in airplane mode.

Questions

Good to know

Does Sona work without an internet connection?

Yes. Speech recognition runs entirely on your phone, so recording and transcribing never need a connection. The AI organizing step is optional — with it off, an on-device rule engine still pulls out dates, amounts and categories.

Is my voice recording uploaded anywhere?

No. Audio never leaves your device. Only transcribed text is sent when cloud organizing is enabled, and you can switch that off at any time in Settings.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Sona has no account system and no server of its own. Your notes are stored locally, and uninstalling the app removes them.

How much does it cost?

Sona is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Which languages does it support?

The interface follows your device language — Chinese on Chinese-language devices, English everywhere else.

Why is the first voice note a download?

The speech recognition model is fetched the first time you use your voice rather than shipped inside the app, which keeps the install small. Once it is on your phone, everything runs offline.
